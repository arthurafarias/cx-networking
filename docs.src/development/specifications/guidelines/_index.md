Pinned engineering guidelines that apply across every SRS. A guideline is not
itself a numbered SRS with its own lifecycle — it stays in force regardless of
which individual specifications are active, implemented, or archived, until
explicitly revised.

- [Testing Convention](testing-convention/) — how the self-hosted test facility
  is structured and what every new module must add.
- [Plugin Folder Convention](plugin-folder-convention/) — where external
  dependencies are allowed to live.
